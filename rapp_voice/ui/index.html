RAPP Voice

Hold a key anywhere on macOS, speak, release, and cleaned-up text appears at your cursor.
Everything here runs on this machine. Audio is captured to a temp file, transcribed by whisper.cpp on localhost, and discarded. The one exception is the opt-in polish hook (say “polish” first): the default hook pipes that transcript to claude -p. Leave it unused, or repoint it at a local model, and nothing leaves the machine.
